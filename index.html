Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

TUC Events Instagram Analytics Dashboard is an internal tool designed to view and analyze Instagram professional account performance.

Information We Access

With appropriate authorization, the dashboard may access Instagram professional account information, profile details, media information, and insights such as reach and engagement.

How We Use Information

Information is used only to display and analyze Instagram account performance and improve internal reporting.

Sharing and Security

We do not sell personal information. Access tokens and authorized account data should be kept secure and accessible only to authorized users.

Data Retention and Deletion

Account data should be retained only as needed for analytics. To request deletion of data associated with this dashboard, contact the dashboard administrator through the organization operating the tool.

Third-Party Services

Instagram and Meta services are subject to their own privacy policies and terms.

Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the administrator responsible for TUC Events Instagram Analytics.
